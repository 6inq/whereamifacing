# Changelog: WhereAmIFacing

## 1.1.1 (2026-10-20)
### Fixed
- The client could crash when the plugin was turned on or off.
- Settings now load and save reliably.

## 1.1.0 (2026-10-17)
- The arrow is drawn on the ground at your character's feet and follows slopes and stairs.

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-17)
- First release: shows which way you face (N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W, NW), in any colour, with an optional compass window.
